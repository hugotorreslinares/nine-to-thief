How to play Nine to Thief

Nine to Thief is an isometric stealth game for browser, mobile and Android TV. You sneak into an office, steal the marked items and dodge your coworkers' line of sight before the one-minute level timer runs out.

Play now

What the game is

You are a masked thief working in an office full of people. In every level you must steal one category of items from desks and corners without any coworker seeing you steal. You get one minute per level and difficulty ramps up: more items and more guards.

It plays in your desktop or mobile browser with nothing to download, and there is also a free Android TV app.

How to steal without getting caught

Find the items highlighted in yellow and walk up to them. Hold the steal button until the bar above your head fills: you cannot move while stealing. If a guard sees you steal, the alarm goes off and you lose the level.

Every coworker watches through a vision cone. When one has you in sight a sweat drop appears over your head; leave their view before you steal. The radar shows where everyone is and which items are left.

Characters and abilities

There are 18 characters: Nine, the hero of the art toy, and 17 original office workers. Each has its own ability: distract others, run faster, go unnoticed or steal quicker. You can throw a decoy to pull gazes away and push other characters and some furniture.

Two-player mode and Android TV

On Android TV you can play two-player co-op on the same TV, each player using their phone as a controller: together you steal the level's items, but if a guard catches either of you, you both lose. Install the TV app from the Android TV page.

Global leaderboard and the art toy

Register a nickname to join the global leaderboard: level reached counts first, then the time you had left. The first two levels are free to play. All 10 levels unlock by tapping the Nine art toy (a collectible figure with an NFC chip) to your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is Nine to Thief free?

Yes, it is free to play in the browser with nothing to download: the first levels are free. All 10 levels unlock with the Nine art toy.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the mobile browser with an on-screen joystick and buttons, and you can add it to your home screen.

Is there an Android TV version?

Yes, there is a free app for Android TV and Google TV, and your phone works as the controller.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes, two-player co-op on the TV with two phones as controllers.

How long is each level?

One minute per level, although some character abilities give extra time.

What is the Nine art toy?

A collectible figure of Nine, the masked thief, with an NFC tag that unlocks all 10 levels and saves your progress.
