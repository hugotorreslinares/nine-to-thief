Nine to Thief: free stealth game where you steal the office without getting caught

Nine to Thief is a free isometric stealth game you play in your browser, on your phone or on Android TV, with nothing to download. Pick one of 16 characters, sneak into the office, steal staplers, pencils, coffee mugs, keyboards and trophies, and dodge your coworkers' line of sight before the one-minute level timer runs out. Play solo or two-player co-op on TV and climb the global leaderboard.

How to play · Android TV · Jugar en español

Is Nine to Thief free?

Yes, it is free to play in the browser with nothing to download: the first two levels are free. All 10 levels unlock with the Nine art toy.

Can I play on mobile and Android TV?

Yes. It runs in the mobile browser, and there is a free Android TV app where your phone works as the controller.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes, two-player co-op on the TV with two phones as controllers.
